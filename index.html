How the numbers work

  • Starting burn: the Mifflin–St Jeor equation × your activity level, plus training and running.
  • Adaptive burn: average calories eaten, corrected for how fast your trend weight is moving (about 7,700 kcal per kg). It takes over gradually as you log more days.
  • Protein: 2.0 g per kg (about 0.9 g per lb) of your goal weight while cutting, or of trend weight otherwise. Fat: 0.7 g per kg of the same. Carbs fill the remaining calories.
  • Pace is capped at 1% of bodyweight per week. Faster than that, more of what you lose comes from muscle.
  • Food estimates from Claude and label photos are approximate. Check what Claude read off a label before you save it.
